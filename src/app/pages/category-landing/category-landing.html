Our Brands

Three unique identities. One shared vision of creativity and craftsmanship.

Zulu

Zulu represents individuality and creativity. Built around the idea of customizable fashion, Zulu allows people to express themselves through unique designs, modern styles, and personalized clothing. It is a brand where fashion meets identity.

Pooboo

Pooboo is dedicated to the joyful world of children. Designed for babies and kids, Pooboo focuses on comfort, safety, and colorful creativity. Every piece is crafted to bring smiles, warmth, and playful fashion for the little ones.

Aurum

Aurum celebrates elegance and timeless beauty. Focused on ornaments and jewelry, Aurum blends tradition with modern design to create pieces that shine with grace and sophistication.
