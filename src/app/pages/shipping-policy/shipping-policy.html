Shipping Policy

ZULU Boutique offers reliable and timely shipping across India. Orders are typically processed within 2–3 business days, excluding weekends and public holidays.

Once dispatched, delivery timelines may vary based on your location and courier partner serviceability. You will receive tracking details as soon as your order ships.

While we aim to meet estimated delivery times, unforeseen delays due to weather, logistics, or high-demand periods may occur. We appreciate your patience during peak sale seasons and festive periods.

If your order has not arrived within the expected timeframe, please reach out to our support team at hello@zuluboutique.com.
