Returns & Exchange

At ZULU Boutique, your satisfaction is our priority. If you receive a damaged, defective, or incorrect item, you may request a return or exchange within 7 days of delivery.

To be eligible, items must be unused, unworn, unwashed, and returned with original tags and packaging intact. We reserve the right to decline returns that do not meet these conditions.

Custom-made pieces, intimate wear, and discounted or sale items are not eligible for return or exchange unless received in a damaged condition.

Once your return request is approved, our support team will guide you through the next steps. Refunds (if applicable) will be processed to the original payment method after quality checks.

For assistance, contact us at hello@zuluboutique.com with your order number and clear images of the item.
